Tidal Reaper

Material: Unknown crystalline alloy

Function: Ceremonial weapon

Location Found: Blackglass Cavern

This blade appears untouched by time, its surface smooth and uncorroded despite centuries of exposure to saltwater. When held to the ear, it emits a faint resonance resembling distant voices or the echo of waves inside a cave. Researchers have debated whether this sound is caused by trapped air chambers within the crystalline structure or by some unknown acoustic property of the material itself. Its seamless construction suggests it was grown rather than forged, possibly shaped slowly through chemical or biological processes rather than hammered into form. Ritual markings near the handle indicate it may have been used in ceremonial performances rather than combat. Some scholars believe it symbolized the harvesting of tides or the cutting away of bad fortune, while others argue it represented authority among coastal leaders. Whatever its true purpose, the Tidal Reaper stands as one of the most mysterious and carefully preserved artifacts in the collection.
